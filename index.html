The mood

Commercial enough for the shelf. Easy enough for the night.

Shake Shake is a ready-to-drink fruit soju from Taunggyi (Aye Thar Yar), balanced for a smooth pour and a light social feel.

The format keeps the serve simple: open the bottle, add ice if you like, shake gently, and pour. No extra mixer, no complicated bar setup.

How to serve

Cold bottle. All flavors. Easy pour.

01 Chill

Keep every flavor cold for the crispest fruit taste.

02 Shake

Gently shake before serving to wake up the fruit notes.

03 Pour

Serve straight or over ice. Mixer is optional.
